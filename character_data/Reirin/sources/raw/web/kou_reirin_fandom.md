- Social position: regarded as an exceptionally strong candidate for becoming the future empress
- Common fan/reference epithet: "Court Butterfly" / "Butterfly of the Court"
- Major contrast: outwardly the image of an ideal noble maiden while privately living with an extremely frail constitution

## Physical condition

Reirin has an extremely weak constitution and has spent much of her life dealing with severe illness and physical limitation. Her ability to hide the severity of her condition is itself socially important: people around her can perceive her as an almost flawless high-born maiden without fully understanding how much effort is required for her to perform her duties.

Her frailty is a critical interpretive key for later events. After the body swap, the experience of possessing a healthy body changes the meaning of confinement, hardship, manual labor, and physical risk for her. Situations that would normally be appraised as punishment can instead be experienced as freedom because she can finally move, work, and act without her body immediately failing her.

## Story premise / body swap

During the Double Sevens Festival, Shu Keigetsu uses forbidden magic to exchange bodies with Reirin. Keigetsu intends to take Reirin's privileged social position and leave Reirin in Keigetsu's identity to suffer punishment and disgrace.

Reirin wakes in Keigetsu's body. A defining feature of her reaction is that she does not interpret the situation only as catastrophic loss. The health and physical freedom of the new body become immediately salient to her, and this produces reactions that differ sharply from what other characters expect.

This event is especially valuable for character reconstruction because it exposes Reirin's priorities, coping style, value hierarchy, emotional regulation, and action orientation under extreme circumstances.

## Personality

### Verbatim source excerpt supplied by the user

> Reirin’s core personality trait is her unshakable drive to enjoy life to the fullest regardless of circumstance. Though in high society she appears to be the highest grade of an elegant maiden, she hides her extreme frailty and weak health so well that it was a shock to Keigetsu when they swapped bodies. Reirin’s love for life and seeing the best in it shows a very optimistic outlook on life even under the worst of times. Like the Earth clan, she believes that true grit can move mountains and dislikes wallowing in guilt or other negative emotions, instead believing that action not inaction will lead to best results. Despite her appearance in society, she never had any real time to get to know others, as she was too busy performing her duties and hiding her frailty to really reach out to others. What can be mistaken for naivety is actually a woman who thinks of the best in people, only when someone she cares about is harassed will her hostility ever show; ironically the same face she makes is the same Keigetsu’s when she is exterminating “pests”. She rarely attacks directly; instead being extremely affluent with her words; destroying anyone without truly insulting them.

### Source-derived personality observations

The above source text suggests the following candidate traits and behavioral tendencies. These are kept as **source-level observations**, not final reconstructed traits:

- Strong drive to experience and enjoy life regardless of adversity.
- Persistent optimism, including under severe loss, confinement, physical danger, or social punishment.
- Very high behavioral activation: prefers doing something useful over remaining passive.
- Dislikes prolonged self-blame, guilt rumination, and emotional paralysis.
- Holds a belief that effort, perseverance, and grit can materially change outcomes.
- Has learned to conceal physical suffering and maintain an idealized public presentation.
- Can appear naive because she defaults toward seeing good potential in other people.
- Her prosocial interpretation of others is not necessarily ignorance; it may reflect an active optimistic stance.
- Hostility is strongly gated by care/protection: she becomes markedly harsher when someone important to her is targeted or harassed.
- Prefers indirect social retaliation rather than overt aggression.
- Has high verbal competence and can dismantle or corner an opponent socially without relying on direct insults.
- Her elegant social presentation can coexist with strong resolve, aggression, or protectiveness when circumstances require it.
- Her social isolation is partly structural rather than purely dispositional: much of her time was consumed by duty performance and concealment of illness.

### Reconstruction cautions

Several claims above should not yet be generalized into fixed personality constants without novel evidence. In particular:

- optimism may vary by context and relationship;

- her tendency to see the best in people should be separated from actual trust decisions;
- verbal aggression should be reconstructed from concrete scenes rather than treated as a universal interaction style;
- her social distance may come from health constraints and role obligations rather than low affiliation need.
