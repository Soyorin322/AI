# Reirin Reconstruction v0.1 Report

## Boundary

Reirin v0.1 uses only the approved speaker-rechecked Volume 1 and Volume 2
files. Their Git blob SHAs are frozen in `manifest.json`. The excluded next
volume was not read, searched, parsed, analyzed, or used.

The snapshot boundary is the end of Volume 2. Source truth, observations,
objective events, evidence, hypotheses, and consolidated state remain separate.
Reirin is always identified as 黃玲琳; `body_identity` records 朱慧月 during the
exchange and 黃玲琳 after restoration.

## Segmentation

Twenty-two source units were selected as compact scene-local passages with
volume, chapter/scene, approximate line span, and semantic anchor locators. They
cover identity/body attribution, health history, warehouse life, representative
choices under danger and responsibility, relationship interactions, the central
crisis, and the end-of-Volume-2 settlement. Speaker labels and relevant local
context are preserved. The volumes were not reduced to a single summary unit and
dialogue lines were not mechanically converted into claims.

## Consolidated coverage

The evidence supports a limited v0.1 covering identity/body separation, severe
frailty and health self-management, qualified emotional/pain suppression,
effort and practical learning, protective role responsibility, living/reparative
atonement, reciprocal friendship motivation, health/freedom versus duty conflict,
and context-sensitive accountability. These are claim records with evidence
links, not a final Persona schema.

## Retained uncertainty

- **Unresolved:** whether leniency toward specific offenders generalizes into a
  broad forgiveness/rehabilitation tendency. Protective anger and demands for
  redress are retained as counterevidence.
- **Unresolved:** whether Reirin's feelings toward Yaoming are romantic rather
  than familial affection, trust, or emerging attraction.
- **Candidate:** whether greater assertiveness after the exchange is a persistent
  character change rather than health-enabled expression or situational behavior.
- **Candidate:** whether one narrator statement about low power ambition is broad
  and stable enough to consolidate.

## Limitations

This is selective representative coverage, not exhaustive sentence-level corpus
encoding. It contains no automatic psychology inference, confidence formula,
contradiction resolver, runtime compiler, final relationship model, or appraisal
engine. No production database or LLM is involved. Later validation material must
not be retroactively treated as construction evidence for this frozen v0.1.
