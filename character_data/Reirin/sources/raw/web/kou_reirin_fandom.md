## Physical condition

Reirin is characterized by an extremely frail constitution. Her poor health places severe limits on normal physical activity and is an important part of her early characterization and later reaction to the body-swap incident.

## Core story premise involving Reirin

During the Double Sevens Festival, Shu Keigetsu uses forbidden magic to exchange bodies with Reirin. Keigetsu intends to take Reirin's privileged position while leaving Reirin to suffer the consequences attached to Keigetsu's identity.

Reirin consequently awakens in Keigetsu's healthier body. Rather than reacting only with despair at the loss of her status, she is strongly affected by the physical freedom of possessing a body that can move without immediately failing her. This contrast between Reirin's former physical limitations and her new body's strength becomes a major driver of the early story.
